Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can the invitation be customised to match our event?

Yes. Every section — names, wording, photos, colours, venues, timeline, music and gift details — is tailored to your celebration. If you need a section we don't offer yet, tell us and we'll build it in.

How does the RSVP work?

Guests reply directly inside the invitation with a single tap. They can confirm how many people are attending, add each guest's name and leave you a message. Every reply appears instantly on your private dashboard.

Will it work on every phone?

Yes. Invitations are built mobile-first and tested on iPhone, Android and tablets. There is no app to install — guests simply open the link in whatever browser they already use.

How do we share the invitation?

You get one link that works everywhere: WhatsApp, SMS, email or social media. You can also generate a personal link for each guest so their name appears on their own invitation.

Can we send personal links to each guest?

Absolutely. Import your guest list, set how many seats each guest is allowed, and every guest receives their own link. Their RSVP is matched to them automatically.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the template and the features you need. Get in touch with a few details about your event and we'll send a tailored quote — no obligation.
